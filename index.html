Privacy policy: Dialpad for PropertyMe

Last updated: 7 October 2026 · Extension: Dialpad for PropertyMe · Publisher: Spark Integrations

Who this is for

This extension is for staff of a property management agency that uses PropertyMe and Dialpad, set up for them by Spark Integrations. It is distributed privately (unlisted) and is not for the general public.

What it reads

When a staff member opens a contact in PropertyMe, the extension reads the contact information already loaded in that page by PropertyMe. An administrator can also load the agency's whole contact list in one go (a "full sync"), which reads the same details for every contact:

  • name, email address and phone numbers
  • the related property addresses, and whether the person is a tenant, owner or supplier
  • PropertyMe's internal reference and record identifiers

The extension only reads these on manager.propertyme.com, using the staff member's existing login. It does not read any other website, and it never changes, adds or deletes anything in PropertyMe.

Where it goes

That information is sent to the agency's own sync service, a private database (Supabase) controlled by the agency and its IT provider. It is used for two things only:

1. keeping the agency's Dialpad contact list current, so incoming calls show who is ringing and about which property

2. showing that contact's call and text history to staff inside PropertyMe

The database is private: only the agency's own services can read it.

Call and text information

Call and text records (time, direction, which staff member, duration, and where Dialpad AI is enabled, a summary and action items) come from the agency's Dialpad account into the same private database. The extension displays them.

Call recordings are never stored, and recording or transcript links are stripped before anything is saved.

What is never collected

  • No browsing history, and no activity on any site other than PropertyMe
  • No passwords or login details. The extension uses the staff member's existing PropertyMe session and never sees their password
  • No data from personal use of the browser

Sharing

The information is not sold, not shared with advertisers, and not used for anything beyond the two purposes above. It is held by the agency and its service providers (Supabase for the database, Dialpad for telephony).

Retention and removal

Records stay in the agency's database while the person is a contact in PropertyMe. The agency can delete any person's records at any time on request, and can delete the whole database when the service ends.

The people whose details these are

Tenants and owners are the agency's clients, and the agency remains responsible for their information under the Australian Privacy Act. Requests for access or correction should go to that agency.

Contact

Questions about the extension: Spark Integrations, admin@sparkintegrations.com.au

Requests to see, correct or remove your personal information go to the agency that holds your records. Spark will act on the agency's instructions.
